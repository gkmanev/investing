# PutPulse Terms of Service Addendum — Agent Fair Use

## Agent usage

Paid Pro access is marketed as unlimited for normal individual use. "Unlimited" does not mean unbounded or unrestricted use. To protect service reliability, prevent abuse, and control automated or excessive consumption, Pro agent usage is subject to fair-use safeguards.

The service currently applies circuit breakers of 60 agent runs in any rolling one-hour period and 200 agent runs per day. We may throttle, delay, limit, suspend, or terminate agent access where usage is abusive, automated beyond permitted use, attempts to bypass limits, materially degrades the service, or creates unreasonable operational cost or security risk.

These safeguards may change as the service evolves. They do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, and they apply in addition to any restrictions in the main Terms of Service.
